Digital marketing strategy

A brand built with strategy.

This project brings key marketing frameworks into one coherent customer journey—from awareness to consideration and conversion.

THE 4 Ps

Marketing mix

Product: fragrance stories. Price: value-led tiers to be finalised. Place: digital-first in Pakistan. Promotion: content, creators, and campaigns.

SWOT

Strategic lens

Strength: memorable storytelling. Opportunity: social commerce. Challenge: trust-building online. Response: transparent details and useful content.

AIDA

Message journey

Attention: bold scent visuals. Interest: note guides. Desire: occasion-led stories. Action: clear enquiry and purchase CTAs.

SEO + CONTENT

Get discovered

Use relevant fragrance keywords, descriptive page titles, helpful scent guides, image alt text, and consistent social content.

Full-funnel thinking

TOFU → MOFU → BOFU

Give people the right message at the right stage.

01 · Awareness

TOFU

Reels, scent mood boards, fragrance myths, seasonal posts, and shareable storytelling to reach new audiences.

02 · Consideration

MOFU

Scent-family guides, comparison content, FAQs, gifting guides, and email/WhatsApp opt-ins to build confidence.

03 · Conversion

BOFU

Clear product details, social proof when available, a simple enquiry path, limited-time campaigns only when genuine, and follow-up service.
